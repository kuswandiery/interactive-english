# STYLE — English Academy

## Brand Personality

English Academy memiliki kepribadian:

- Friendly
- Professional
- Motivational
- Modern
- Energetic
- Trustworthy

---

## Brand Tone

Gunakan bahasa:

- Ramah
- Jelas
- Singkat
- Positif
- Memotivasi
- Profesional

Hindari bahasa yang terlalu kaku.

---

## Writing Style

Gunakan kalimat pendek dan mudah dipahami.

Contoh:

"Improve your English. Build your confidence."

Hindari:

"Through our comprehensive educational methodology, students will be able to..."

---

## Hero Copy

Headline:

"Speak English With Confidence"

Description:

"Build your English skills with practical lessons, expert tutors, and a learning experience designed around your goals."

Primary CTA:

"Start Learning"

Secondary CTA:

"Explore Courses"

---

## Button Style

Gunakan kata kerja yang jelas.

Contoh:

- Start Learning
- Explore Courses
- View Course
- Learn More
- Join Now
- Continue Learning
- Take Quiz
- View Certificate

Hindari:

- Click Here
- Submit
- More

---

## Icon Style

Gunakan icon sederhana dan konsisten.

Contoh:

- Book
- GraduationCap
- Users
- Clock
- Award
- CheckCircle
- Play
- Star
- MessageCircle

---

## Image Style

Gunakan gambar yang berhubungan dengan:

- English learning
- Students
- Online classroom
- Tutor
- Conversation
- Laptop learning
- Education

Hindari gambar yang tidak relevan.

---

## Card Style

Card menggunakan:

- Rounded corners
- Soft border
- Soft shadow
- Clear spacing
- Strong heading
- Supporting information

---

## Empty State

Gunakan pesan yang ramah.

Contoh:

"No courses yet"

"Start exploring our courses and begin your English learning journey."

CTA:

"Explore Courses"

---

## Error State

Gunakan:

"Something went wrong."

"Please try again."

---

## Success State

Gunakan:

"Great job!"

"Your quiz has been completed successfully."

---

## Loading State

Gunakan:

- Skeleton
- Spinner
- Loading indicator

Loading harus terasa natural dan tidak mengganggu.
